5. Contacting Me by Email or Phone

If you email or call me, I process the data you provide, for example your name, contact details, company and the content of your message, in order to handle your enquiry.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your enquiry relates to a contract or to steps prior to entering into a contract; otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is answering enquiries, including those from contact persons of companies.

Email providers: The mailbox jb@bachent.com is hosted by IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Str. 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany. Incoming emails are stored there and forwarded to a Gmail account provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, where I read and answer them. My replies are sent via the IONOS mail servers. Google may transfer data to Google LLC in the USA; Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR).

Retention: I delete your data once it is no longer needed to handle your enquiry, unless statutory retention obligations apply (in particular under German commercial and tax law) or the data is needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

7. Booking a Call (Google Calendar)

The “Book a Call” and “Choose a Time” buttons are plain links to a booking page provided by Google Ireland Limited (Google Calendar appointment schedules). Visiting this website does not send any data to Google; data is only processed once you open the booking page.

Data: On the booking page you enter your first and last name, email address, phone number and company, optionally a note, and choose a time slot. The booking is stored as an event in my Google Calendar. Google sends you and me a confirmation email with a calendar invitation and creates a Google Meet link for the call.
Purpose: scheduling and holding the call you requested, and following up on it.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps prior to entering into a contract, taken at your request). If you book on behalf of a company, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is communicating with prospective business partners.
Required information: name, email address, phone number and company are required to book. Without them, a booking is not possible; you can always contact me by email or phone instead.

Google’s processing: When you use the booking page or join a Google Meet call, Google also processes technical and usage data, such as your IP address, device information and, where applicable, cookies, and may use it for its own purposes as described in the Google Privacy Policy. Google may transfer data to Google LLC in the USA; Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR).
Retention: I delete booking data once it is no longer needed for the call and any follow-up, unless a business relationship results from it or statutory retention obligations apply. You can cancel or reschedule via the link in your confirmation email.

8. Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). To exercise these rights, simply email jb@bachent.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the EU member state of your habitual residence, place of work or the place of the alleged infringement. The authority competent for me is the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany, www.lda.bayern.de.

9. Right to Object (Art. 21 GDPR)

Where I process your data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you have the right to object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. I will then no longer process your data unless I can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds for the processing which override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. An email to jb@bachent.com is sufficient.

10. No Automated Decision-Making

I do not use automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.
